## 2. Why PeopleOps Was Built

### Section Heading (H2)
### The operational gap between raw data and fair leadership review.

### Body Narrative
In modern software, technology services, and consulting companies, operational work takes place across a dozen different cloud platforms. HR records live in HRMS tools, attendance is captured by biometric hardware, team communication occurs in Microsoft Teams, and technical execution advances through GitHub.

While companies collect enormous amounts of operational data, leadership teams often lack a unified, accurate view of daily operations. Operations leaders and HR heads spend hours each week manually stitching together conflicting reports. Even worse, decisions regarding resource allocation, capacity, and project timelines are frequently made using incomplete or delayed information.

Codework created **PeopleOps Intelligence** to bridge this gap. We engineered a centralized [centralised] platform that connects directly to the tools your teams already use, reconciles user identities, applies approved business rules, and delivers transparent, KPI-driven visibility designed specifically for human-led management review.

## 3. Our Operating Philosophy & Product Principles

### Section Heading (H2)
### The principles guiding our software design.

Every capability in PeopleOps Intelligence is built around four fundamental principles confirmed in our architecture and requirements:

#### 1. Insight for Human Review, Never Automated Decisions
We believe software should inform leadership conversations, not replace them. PeopleOps is engineered strictly to support management review. It does not automate disciplinary actions, compute salary deductions, or generate algorithmic termination decisions. Data provides context; human leaders provide judgement.

#### 2. Transparency Over Black-Box Algorithms
Trust requires clarity. When PeopleOps calculates performance bands or task completion rates, it does so using approved, documented business rules and visible weightings. When source data is missing, incomplete, or delayed, our software explicitly flags the gap rather than presenting misleading estimates.

#### 3. Respect for Employee Privacy
High-performing organizations [organisations] thrive on mutual trust, not constant surveillance. PeopleOps deliberately rejects invasive monitoring technologies. We do not record keystrokes, capture desktop screenshots, or monitor private employee messages. We focus strictly on objective operational outputs: project tasks, pull requests, and verified attendance.

#### 4. Least-Privilege Governance & Security
Workforce records are sensitive. PeopleOps enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) so that leaders only see the operational data pertinent to their role. Every access event to sensitive information is recorded in traceable audit logs to ensure institutional compliance.

## 4. Documented Core Strengths & Differentiators

* **Cross-Source Identity Reconcilation:** Solves the challenge of matching an employee's HR record with their Microsoft 365 identity, biometric logs, and GitHub handle.
* **Unified Attendance & Delivery View:** Connects physical attendance and remote presence directly with actual project and code delivery metrics.
* **Conversational Operational Queries ("Ask Tara"):** Enables leaders to query their workforce data using natural language within secure, role-governed boundaries.
* **Data Freshness & Sync Integrity:** Features continuous integration health monitoring to verify data accuracy and timestamps before presenting dashboards to management.
