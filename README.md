## Key Strategic Pillars Applied

1. **Zero "Bossware" / Surveillance Connotations:**  
   Completely eliminated words like *spy*, *surveillance*, *idle policing*, or *keystroke tracking*. Framed all copy around *operational visibility*, *workload balance*, and *fair, evidence-based management review*.
2. **Strict Adherence to Confirmed Scope:**  
   Every feature, integration, and safeguard is mapped directly to the BRD v1.0. All out-of-scope capabilities (e.g., payroll, recruitment, automated discipline) are explicitly disclosed.
3. **Calm, High-Converting B2B Tone:**  
   Avoided tired AI buzzwords (*"revolutionizing"*, *"game-changer"*). Created actionable, respectful messaging tailored for British and American business leaders.
