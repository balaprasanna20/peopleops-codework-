# Deliverable 7: CTA Strategy & Recommendations Matrix

**Product:** PeopleOps Intelligence  
**Core Goal:** Maximize high-intent demo bookings among executive decision-makers (C-Suite, HR Heads, Delivery Heads) while providing low-friction exploration pathways for technical evaluators.  
**Constraint:** Do not advertise a "Free Trial" or "Free Pilot" unless commercially confirmed.

---

## 1. Global CTA Hierarchy & Decision Framework

| Decision Stage | Visitor Mindset | Primary CTA Intent | Recommended Phrasing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top of Funnel (Awareness)** | "I have scattered operational data and want to see what this software actually looks like." | Low-commitment exploration & product tours. | **"See How PeopleOps Works"** / **"Explore Product Views"** |
| **Mid Funnel (Evaluation)** | "We use Teams and GitHub. Does this integrate cleanly and respect employee privacy?" | Architecture validation & methodology review. | **"Check Integration Architecture"** / **"Review Privacy Safeguards"** |
| **Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)** | "We need to replace manual reporting spreadsheets for our leadership team." | Direct, scheduled consultative interaction. | **"Book a Live Demo"** / **"Schedule Your Walkthrough"** |

---

## 3. Micro-Copy & Trust Anchors Near CTAs

To minimize form abandonment and conversion friction, pair high-priority CTA buttons with one of the following verified micro-reassurances:

1. **Below Demo Request Form:**
   > *"No pushy sales calls. A personalized 20-minute walkthrough tailored to your existing software stack."*
2. **Below Executive Hero:**
   > *"Role-based access. Full audit trails. Zero keystroke logging or desktop screenshots."*
3. **Near Form Email Input:**
   > *"Please use your corporate work email so we can verify connected software compatibility."*
