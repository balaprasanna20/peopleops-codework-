## 1. Reference Website Competitive & UX Analysis

We analyzed the live websites of **Worklytics** (`worklytics.co`), **Hatica** (`hatica.io`), and **ActivTrak** (`activtrak.com`) to extract conversion frameworks, content architecture, and strategic pitfalls to avoid.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Position PeopleOps as the *single source of truth for fair management review*—uniting HR, attendance, and project execution. |
| **Trust & Employee Privacy** | Dedicated "Protect Employee Privacy" section highlighting pseudonymization and zero content inspection. | "Privacy by Design" highlight; developer focus time protection. | Contrasts workforce analytics against intrusive surveillance; RBAC highlighted. | **Direct, Uncompromising Reassurance:** Feature a dedicated Trust & Safeguards section. ## 3. Brand Voice & Editorial Guidelines

### The PeopleOps Voice Principles
1. **Calm and Grounded:** We speak like an experienced operational director or trusted management advisor. We never shout, use exaggerated hype, or rely on artificial urgency.
2. **Objective and Fair:** We advocate for human context. Data provides the starting point for constructive conversations—never an automated verdict.
3. **Transparent and Precise:** If data is missing or systems are out of sync, we show it clearly. We don't pretend algorithms can replace leadership judgement.
4. **Respectful of People:** We build tools that support teams, not police them. We never use language that implies covert surveillance.

### Forbidden Words vs. Preferred Vocabulary

| Do NOT Use | Use Instead | Context / Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Employee monitoring / tracking* | **Operational visibility / Workforce clarity / Activity insight** | Prevents bossware/surveillance connotations. |
| *Snooping / Spyware / Surveillance* | **Management review / Fair evaluation** | Clarifies ethical product boundaries. |
| *Idle time policing / Slacking* | **Capacity balance / Focus availability** | Emphasizes team well-being and resource distribution. |
| *Game-changer / Revolutionizing* | **Practical, dependable, structured** | Eliminates tired SaaS buzzwords. |
| *Seamless experience / One-stop shop* | **Connected workflow / Unified view** | Replaces generic marketing filler with clear utility. |
| *Unlock the power of* | **Access / Review / Identify** | Direct, action-oriented verbs. |

### UK vs. US Spelling & Terminology Guide
To ensure seamless reading across the UK (primary market) and USA (secondary market):
* **Spelling:** Use clean international English. When publishing in the UK, prioritize **-ise** (*prioritise, optimise, organise*), **-our** (*behaviour, colour*), and **programme**. When localizing for the US, toggle to **-ize** (*prioritize, optimize, organize*), **-or** (*behavior, color*), and **program**.
* **Workplace Terms:**
  * UK: *Holiday / Annual Leave* | US: *Paid Time Off (PTO) / Vacation*
  * UK: *Managing Director (MD)* | US: *President / Managing Partner*
  * UK: *Direct Reports / Team Members* | US: *Employees / Staff*
