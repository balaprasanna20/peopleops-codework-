## 2. What to Expect During Your Demo

### Section Heading (H2)
### What happens after you request a walkthrough?

We respect your time. Our demonstrations are consultative, tailored operational reviews—not scripted, high-pressure sales pitches.

1. **Prompt Confirmation:** Our team reviews your company profile and existing software stack (e.g., Microsoft 365, GitHub, GreytHR) within one business day.
2. **Focused 20-Minute Session:** We demonstrate live dashboard views configured to your industry (IT services, consulting, or software development) using realistic dummy data.
3. **Architecture & Governance Review:** We discuss cross-source identity matching, data validation rules, and role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure alignment with your internal security standards.
4. **Transparent Next Steps:** We provide clear documentation, integration requirements, and commercial guidelines for your internal evaluation.
