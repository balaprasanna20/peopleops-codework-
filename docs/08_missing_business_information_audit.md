# Deliverable 9: Missing Business Information & Pre-Publication Audit

**Document Purpose:** Identifies critical commercial, technical, and legal details omitted from the Business Requirements Document (BRD v1.0) that require formal confirmation from Codework management prior to public website launch.  
**Impact on Publication:** Public pages currently contain clean placeholder phrasing or omit these claims to prevent legal liability or commercial misrepresentation.

---

## 1. Commercial & Pricing Model
* [ ] **Pricing Structure:** Is PeopleOps priced per employee per month (PEPM), per active user/seat, in tiered tiers (e.g., Growth, Pro, Enterprise), or via annual custom licensing?
* [ ] **Free Trial / Proof-of-Concept (PoC) Policy:** Does Codework offer a 14-day free trial, a guided 30-day proof-of-concept, or is onboarding exclusively conducted via paid pilot contracts? *(Website copy currently restricts CTAs to "Book a Live Demo" and avoids unconfirmed "Free Trial" claims).*
* [ ] **Minimum Contract Commitments:** Are agreements month-to-month or annual minimum commitments? Is there a minimum employee threshold (e.g., 50 seats)?

---

## 2. Infrastructure, Hosting & Data Residency
* [ ] **Cloud Hosting Provider:** Where is PeopleOps Intelligence hosted? (e.g., Microsoft Azure UK South / US East, AWS London / N. Virginia, or Google Cloud)?
* [ ] **Data Residency Guarantees:** Can UK clients guarantee their workforce and biometric sync data remains strictly within the UK/EU to satisfy UK GDPR requirements?
* [ ] **On-Premise / Private Cloud Deployments:** Is PeopleOps strictly multi-tenant SaaS, or can enterprise clients deploy it in their private Azure/AWS tenant or on-premises?

---

## 3. Compliance, Privacy & Security Certifications
* [ ] **Formal Legal Certifications:** Does Codework or the PeopleOps infrastructure currently hold formal certifications such as:
  * ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security Management)
  * SOC 2 Type II
  * Cyber Essentials / Cyber Essentials Plus (UK)
  * HIPAA / US State Privacy alignment
  *(Note: All marketing claims regarding these specific acronyms have been intentionally withheld from public copy until formal certificates are on file).*
* [ ] **Data Processing Agreement (DPA):** Is a standardized [standardised in UK] GDPR/DPA document and Cookie Policy ready for public linking in the website footer?

---

## 4. Supported Source Systems & Custom Connectors
* [ ] **Biometric Hardware Compatibility:** Which biometric hardware manufacturers/formats are supported out-of-the-box (e.g., ZKTeco, Matrix, Suprema, generic CSV/API file upload)?
* [ ] **New Customer Integrations:** Are Jira, GitLab, Bitbucket, Slack, and Google Workspace on the immediate roadmap, or are they planned for future releases?
* [ ] **API Access for Customers:** Do customers receive direct API access to export PeopleOps KPI data into external business intelligence tools (e.g., PowerBI, Tableau)?

---

## 5. Corporate Contact & Legal Entity Details
* [ ] **Registered Legal Entity Name:** Is the contracting entity "Codework Ltd" (UK), "Codework Inc." (US), or another corporate entity?
* [ ] **Registered Office Address:** Exact registered address and company registration number for the website footer and legal imprint.
* [ ] **Telephone Numbers:** Official UK and US inbound phone numbers for enterprise support and commercial enquiries.
* [ ] **Official Email Inboxes:** Confirm aliases for:
  * Inbound Demos: `demo@codework.com` or `sales@codework.com`
  * Support & Integrations: `support@codework.com`
  * Privacy & Data Protection Officer: `privacy@codework.com`

---

## 6. Executive Team & About Us Profiles
* [ ] **Leadership Bios & Photos:** Names, verified job titles, and LinkedIn profile links for the founders, CTO, Product Owner, and QA Lead mentioned in the RACI chart.
* [ ] **Company History & Scale:** Year Codework was founded, total team headcount, and key engineering milestones.

---
